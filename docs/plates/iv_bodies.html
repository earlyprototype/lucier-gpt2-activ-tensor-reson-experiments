Five sentences becoming one shape

Each body is one sentence, fed back through a language model over and over. Five unrelated sentences, each fed back through the same model sixty times. In three of the four models they arrive at the same body. The separation quoted here is a score of the angle between two states: 0 when they point the same way, 2 when they point opposite ways, and close to 1 for two unrelated directions. That separation halves in GPT-2 small, from 0.16 to 0.08, falls by three quarters in Pythia 160M, from 0.038 to 0.009, and in GPT-2 medium collapses from 0.043 to 0.0002, which is to say the five states end up pointing the same way to within the width of the measurement. Whatever the sentences were about stops mattering. What is left is the shape of the model.

Pythia 410M is the exception and is worth watching for that reason. Its five bodies start further apart than any other model's and end further apart still. One architecture out of four does not do this, which is a caution against reading the other three as a law.

The lamp colours are arbitrary labels, one per sentence, held constant so an individual body can be followed through its development. The rotation is a display choice; the deformation is entirely the model's.

Watch the silhouette and the ripple separately: the strongest of the 48 components are drawn as the broad swells of the body, and the weakest as its fine surface ripple. They do not settle together: the gross form stops changing well before the fine detail does, which is the geometric face of something the numbers already said, that these states fix their direction early and keep adjusting quietly for a long time after.

Honesty

A body is 48 numbers rendered through a fixed set of 48 surface patterns, one pattern per number, and every number is given equal weight so the shape shows all of them rather than only the loudest two. The 48 are each model's own directions of greatest variation, so bodies compare fairly within one model and not across models.

That equalising is a display choice and it changes apparent distances, so the separation figures above are measured on the raw state vectors instead, not on anything drawn here. Two earlier versions of this page reported the opposite result, because each measured separation in its own display coordinates, and two different display choices gave two opposite conclusions.

Bodies that look alike may still differ along the directions not drawn.
